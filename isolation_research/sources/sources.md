Verification values:

- Fetched: the page was opened on 2026-09-30 and title, author, and date were
  confirmed.
- Fetched, undated: the page was opened but shows no publication date. Living
  documentation is dated "living, accessed 2026-09-30".
- Index only: the title, date, and URL come from the author's own index page or
  a conference listing. The item itself was not read.
- Snippet only: seen in search results but not opened. Treat as unverified.
- Entries added or upgraded by the third session were fetched on 2026-10-01;
  their verification cell says so.
